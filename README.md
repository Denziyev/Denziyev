## About

I am a Computer Science graduate (ASOIU, 2025) currently pursuing an MSc in
Artificial Intelligence and High-Performance Computing Technologies at the University of Turin.
My undergraduate research at the ASOIU High Performance Computing Center focused on
**part-of-speech tagging for Azerbaijani**, a morphologically rich, low-resource language.

Before moving into research, I spent over two years as a **.NET back-end engineer** in banking and fintech,
building card, payment and account services with .NET, Oracle PL/SQL, Kafka and Redis.
I bring that engineering discipline to research: clean implementations, reproducible experiments,
and attention to computational cost.

**Research interests:** Machine Learning · Natural Language Processing · Low-resource languages ·
Explainable AI · Parallel & High-Performance Computing
